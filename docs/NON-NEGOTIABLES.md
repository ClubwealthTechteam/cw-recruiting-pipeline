# Non-negotiables (Wednesday pre-interview)

Source: "CW | Recruiting Process" sheet, WEDNESDAY tab (read only; Recruitment owns it). Noted by Miguel, 2026-10-30.
They are read out at the 2:00 PM PT pre-interview. Applicants who disagree may leave Zoom; that is
**Uninterested | Left Zoom**, not Did Not Complete.

## Experience
- Solid experience for every company. **Experience with a US-based company is required** (a US licensed realtor where the role needs it). No beginners.

## Basic
- Direct client, not an agency. Once offered the job, ideally start the same week.
- Full-time, long-term. Not freelance, part-time or project-based.
- Work hours in Pacific time only (night shift for most applicants).
- Time Doctor time tracking; clocking in and out is mandatory. Paid by the hour, 40 hours a week.
- Camera on during work hours, facing forward. Working webcam and noise-cancelling headset.
- Professional dress code (head to chest). Clients may join the Zoom helpdesk.
- No phone use during work hours.
- Pay through the applicant's own verified Wise account only. A country where Wise is restricted means we cannot pay them.

## Strict: communication and tech
- English proficiency: professional, neutral accent, fluent.
- Internet at least **50 Mbps up and down**, on **Ethernet (LAN)**. Not Wi-Fi only.
- Reliable headset and working camera.
- Computer: **i5 or better** (tech check also accepts Intel i3 12th gen+ or Ryzen 5 5500+) and **8 GB RAM or more**. Checked with dxdiag.

## After passing (main interview, 3:30 PM PT, and Thursday)
- Pre-onboarding Thursday 6:00 AM PT on the CW Zoom. No lates.
- Two-week probation commitment. Any emergency in those two weeks must be communicated (Slack or Facebook).
- On their work computer and work station; tech issues fixed, 50 Mbps with LAN.
- Google forms and the new-hire agreement completed before 6:00 AM PT Thursday.

## Where the CRM uses this
- Stage reasons: Not Fit presets (internet below 50 Mbps, equipment not suitable, communication skills,
  experience does not match) and Uninterested | Left Zoom presets (left after the work rules were explained).
- Not used by the screening routine yet. The form gives internet speed and US-client experience, so these
  two could be checked before the invite if Recruitment wants that.
